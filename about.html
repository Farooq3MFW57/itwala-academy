Who We Are

ITwala Academy is a technology learning academy focused on Microsoft skills, cloud fundamentals, data tools, collaboration platforms, and practical IT confidence. We support beginners, professionals, and teams through clear instruction and guided practice.

Our mission is to make quality IT education accessible, practical, and career-focused. Every course is designed to help learners understand the concepts, practice the tools, and apply new skills in real work scenarios.
